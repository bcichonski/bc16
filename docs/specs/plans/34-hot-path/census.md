## 5. How the "after #17" view is modelled

#17 is not implemented. The model removes the runtime-internal `peek16`/`poke16` calls made by the `stackvar*`, `stackoffs*` and `stackhead*` helpers (the per-variable-access path; the counts are in section 2) and keeps every other use (codegen `#`/comparisons/prologue, library `asm` code, mseek, ...). The removed calls are subtracted at today's per-use cost; whatever #17 puts in their place is not modelled. Option totals are then computed over the remaining uses.
